Meet Momo

Momo Bakehouse started as a small home kitchen project in 2019, when our founder began baking sourdough loaves for friends and neighbors in District 3. What started as a weekend hobby quickly became a passion, and soon enough, the kitchen was too small and the orders were too many. We found a cozy shophouse on a quiet alley, set up our ovens, and opened the doors.

The name "Momo" comes from the Vietnamese word for a soft, round dumpling, and we think it captures exactly what we are about: simple, comforting, lovingly made. We believe that the best baked goods do not need fancy techniques or complicated ingredients. They need good butter, fresh flour, ripe fruit, and someone who genuinely cares about getting it right.

Every single item on our menu is made by hand, from scratch, every morning. Our croissants take 72 hours to make. Our sourdough starter is over four years old. Our cakes are baked to order, never sitting in a fridge waiting. We source our flour from a local mill in the Mekong Delta, our butter from a small dairy in Dalat, and our fruits from the seasonal markets around Saigon.

We are still a small team. You will probably see the same faces every time you visit, and that is exactly how we like it. We know our regulars by name, we remember your usual order, and we always save the last croissant for the person who really needs it. If you have not visited us yet, we hope you will soon. The door is always open and the coffee is always fresh.

Handmade

Made with Love
